# History of Artificial Intelligence

## 1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence has developed through several
periods of progress and challenges.

The development of AI has not always been consistent.
It has experienced periods of rapid growth as well as
periods of reduced interest and investment.

## 2. AI Boom

An AI boom refers to a period when interest, research,
development, and investment in Artificial Intelligence
increase significantly.

During these periods, new technologies and approaches
create opportunities for AI development.

## 3. AI Winter

An AI winter refers to a period when enthusiasm and
investment in Artificial Intelligence decrease.

This can happen when AI systems do not meet expectations
or when available technology is not sufficient to support
the expected results.

## 4. Development of Modern AI

Modern AI has benefited from several important factors:

- Bigger datasets: More data is available for training
  Machine Learning models.
- Faster computers: Improved computing capabilities
  support more complex calculations.
- Open-source packages: Developers and researchers
  can access tools and libraries more easily.
- Neural network architectures: A wider range of
  architectures supports different learning tasks.

## Key Takeaways

- AI development has experienced both booms and winters.
- Data availability contributes to modern AI development.
- Computing improvements make complex models more practical.
- Open-source tools support AI research and development.
