# Modern AI Applications

## 1. Introduction

Artificial Intelligence is widely used in everyday life.
It helps systems analyze information, recognize patterns,
make predictions, and support decision-making.

## 2. Applications of AI

### 2.1 Transportation

AI can support transportation systems by analyzing traffic
conditions and historical data to estimate travel times.

Examples:
- Google Maps and Waze use traffic information to help
  determine suitable routes.
- Ride-sharing platforms such as Uber and Lyft can adjust
  prices based on supply and demand.

### 2.2 Social Media

AI is used to personalize social media experiences.

Examples:
- Recommending relevant content.
- Suggesting people or groups to connect with.
- Delivering targeted advertisements.
- Recognizing images and analyzing sentiment.

### 2.3 Natural Language Processing

Natural Language Processing (NLP) allows computers to
process and understand human language.

Examples:
- Voice assistants such as Siri and Alexa.
- Voice recognition.
- Systems that respond to spoken commands.

### 2.4 Computer Vision

Computer Vision enables computers to interpret visual
information from images and videos.

Examples:
- Image classification.
- Face recognition.
- Object detection.
- Real-time detection for self-driving cars.

### 2.5 Medical Diagnosis

AI can assist medical professionals by analyzing
information that supports medical diagnosis.

### 2.6 Robotics

AI can be integrated into robotic systems to support
tasks that require perception, learning, or decision-making.

## 3. Importance of AI Applications

AI applications help automate tasks, analyze large
amounts of information, and support faster decisions.

However, the quality of an AI system depends on factors
such as data quality and the methods used to develop it.

## Key Takeaways

- AI is used in transportation, social media, healthcare,
  robotics, and language processing.
- Computer Vision focuses on visual information.
- NLP focuses on human language.
- AI can support prediction, recognition, and automation.
